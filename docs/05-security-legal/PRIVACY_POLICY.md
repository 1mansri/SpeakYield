## 1. Who we are
Speak Yield ("we", "us") operates a voice-first agri-commerce platform enabling farmers and other participants in India to buy agricultural inputs and sell produce using voice. Data controller: **[Legal entity name, address, India]**. Grievance/Data Protection contact: **[name, email, phone]**.

## 2. What we collect
- **Account data:** phone number, name, role (farmer/buyer/dealer/delivery), preferred language, and your location/district (for hyperlocal matching).
- **Voice data:** audio recordings of your requests and the transcripts/intent derived from them.
- **Transaction data:** listings, orders, amounts, delivery details, and payment references.
- **KYC data (business accounts):** identity/verification information, processed via our payment partner (Razorpay). We store verification status and references, **not raw document images**.
- **Device & usage data:** app/technical logs needed to run and secure the service.

## 3. Why we use it
- To understand your spoken requests and turn them into orders/listings.
- To match you with nearby buyers, sellers, input dealers, and delivery partners.
- To process payments and payouts and issue invoices.
- To provide support, notifications, and grievance redressal.
- To improve accuracy and safety of the service.
- To meet legal obligations (tax, KYC, records).

## 4. Voice recordings & AI processing (important)
Your voice is processed by automated speech-recognition and AI language services to understand what you want. **Some of these providers process data outside India** (see Section 6). We ask for your **explicit consent** before using voice features, and we **read your request back to you for confirmation before anything is ordered or paid**.

## 5. Payments
Payments are handled by **Razorpay** over UPI. We do **not** store your card or UPI credentials — only references needed to track your order and payment.

## 6. Sharing & cross-border transfer
We share data only as needed to run the service:
- **Speech/AI providers** (e.g. Deepgram/Wispr, OpenAI/Gemini) — to transcribe and understand your voice. **These may process data outside India**; we transfer only with your consent and under data-protection agreements.
- **Payment partner** (Razorpay) — to process payments/KYC.
- **Messaging providers** (SMS/WhatsApp/IVR) — to send OTPs and updates.
- **Delivery partners / counterparties** — the minimum needed to fulfil your order.
- **Authorities** — where legally required.
We do **not** sell your personal data. Your primary data is stored in **India**.

## 7. How long we keep it
We keep data only as long as needed or legally required — for example, short retention for raw audio, and longer retention for tax/payment records. See our retention schedule (summarised for users) and [DATA_RETENTION.md](./DATA_RETENTION.md).

## 8. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)
You can **access, correct, or delete** your personal data, **withdraw consent**, and **raise a grievance**. Contact **[grievance contact]**. Some records (e.g. tax/KYC) may be retained for the legally required minimum even after deletion, in a limited form.

## 9. Security
We use encryption, access controls, and other safeguards ([SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)). No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and authorities of breaches as required by law.

## 10. Children
The service is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children. **[Confirm age policy.]**
